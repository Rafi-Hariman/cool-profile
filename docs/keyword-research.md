# Keyword Research (Technopreneurship Exercise)

This document separates **implementation keywords** (already used in the site
copy and metadata) from **research results** (to be filled later with real
Google Keyword Planner and Google Trends data).

> No search volume, CPC, trend, or competition figure in this document is
> real data. Everything numeric is either `N/A` (not yet collected) or an
> explicit hypothesis. Do not treat hypotheses as findings.

## 1. Product definition

**Jasa pembuatan sistem informasi dan aplikasi web custom.**

The service covers: sistem informasi, aplikasi web, aplikasi Android,
dashboard, sistem transaksi, booking & reservasi, integrasi sistem
(WhatsApp / payment gateway / AI), and fully custom systems.

## 6. Method

1. **Google Search** — inspect the SERP and competing landing pages for each
   candidate keyword; note whether results are directories, agencies,
   freelancers, or informational content.
2. **Google Keyword Planner** — collect search-volume ranges and ad
   competition. Record the exact location, language, and date of the export.
3. **Google Trends** — compare relative interest between terms. Record the
   region, time range, and comparison set.
4. Keep **screenshots** of each source, with the visible date/region/filter.
5. **Do not invent metrics.** If a tool does not expose a number, enter `N/A`.

---

## 7. Initial hypotheses (NOT findings)

These are starting assumptions to be confirmed or rejected by the research
above. They are **not** evidence and must not be presented as facts.

- H1: Transactional terms (e.g. "jasa pembuatan …") convert better than broad
  informational terms for a service landing page.
- H2: Local qualifiers ("… Yogyakarta") have lower volume but higher intent
  and less generic competition than national terms.
- H3: "jasa pembuatan sistem informasi" and "jasa pembuatan aplikasi web" are
  the two strongest primary candidates for the current product definition.
- H4: Need-based long-tail terms (kasir, booking, tagihan) are best served by
  dedicated content or service detail pages, not the homepage.
